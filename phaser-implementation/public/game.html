How to play

  • You're randomly assigned as the Cop (green) or the Robber (red) when you join. A third player joining spectates instead.
  • The Robber is trying to reach the honey node (yellow) without getting caught.
  • Click a node connected to yours by a line to pick your move, then press Commit Move.
  • Both players move at the same time - you won't see their move until you've committed yours.
  • The Cop wins by landing on the Robber's node. The Robber wins by reaching the honey.
